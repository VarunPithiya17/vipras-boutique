    Good to
    know

    How long does stitching take?

    Most blouses and kurtas are ready in 7 working days. Ari work and bridal pieces take 10–14 days. Need it sooner? Ask about express stitching when you book.

    Can I bring my own fabric?

    Of course. Bring your saree blouse piece or suit fabric, or choose from the studio's silks, cottons, ikat and brocades.

    Do you take bulk and wedding orders?

    Yes, family wedding wardrobes, twinning sets and bulk orders for events are a speciality. Book a bridal consultation to plan timelines.

    What if the fit isn't right?

    Every piece includes a trial and free alterations within 15 days of delivery.

    Do you ship outside Pune?

    Yes, we ship across India. Send your measurements through the website and we'll stitch to them.
