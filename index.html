How we stitch

From a conversation to your perfect fit.

No two bodies are the same, so no two Vipra's pieces are either. Five unhurried steps, every single time.

01

Consult

Bring a picture, a fabric or just an occasion. We sketch the neckline, sleeve and finish with you.

02

Choose fabric

Pick from silks, cottons, ikat and brocades in-store, or bring your own saree piece.

03

Measure

Twenty-two measurements, saved on file so your next order fits without another visit.

04

Hand-finish

Ari work, latkans, piping and hand-rolled hems, done by the same hands for over a decade.

05

Trial & deliver

A final trial, free tweaks, then delivered to your door anywhere in Pune or shipped India-wide.
